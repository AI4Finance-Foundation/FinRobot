NVDA 的 WACC 10.2% 是怎么算的？
⚙ compute/wacc → NVDA
WACC = 10.2%，推导过程：

Cost of Equity (CAPM):
CoE = Rf + β × ERP = 4.3% + 1.1 × 5.5% = 10.35% CAPM

WACC:
= E/V × CoE + D/V × Rd × (1-T)
= 0.95 × 10.35% + 0.05 × 5.0% × 79%
= 10.2% Calculated

NVDA 债务比率极低（D/V=5%），WACC 几乎等于 Cost of Equity。
