NVDA 的 WACC 10.2% 是怎么算的？
⚙ Calling compute/wacc with NVDA financials...
NVDA 的 WACC 是 10.2%，计算如下：

Cost of Equity (CAPM):
CoE = Rf + Beta * ERP
= 4.3% + 1.1 * 5.5% = 10.35% CAPM

WACC:
WACC = E/V * CoE + D/V * Rd * (1-T)
= 0.95 * 10.35% + 0.05 * 5.0% * (1-21%)
= 10.2% Calculated

NVDA 的债务比率极低（D/V=5%），所以 WACC 几乎等于 Cost of Equity。
