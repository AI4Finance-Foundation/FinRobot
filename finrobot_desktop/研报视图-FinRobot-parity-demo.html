02 Company Overview Business · Segments · Geography · Moat

Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables and accessories worldwide. Founded in 1976 and headquartered in Cupertino, California, Apple operates through five reportable segments: iPhone (52% of revenue), Services (25%), Wearables/Home/Accessories (10%), Mac (8%), and iPad (5%). The company's competitive moat is anchored by a vertically integrated hardware-software-services ecosystem with 2.2 billion active devices and a Services attach rate exceeding 1 billion paid subscriptions across App Store, iCloud+, Apple Music, Apple TV+, and AppleCare.
